> **Date:** Saturday, 26th September

## 🎯 Tracks

Pick a track matching your idea — or blend buyer, seller, and merchant tooling:

1. **Storefront Experience**
   - Build better ways to discover, explore, and buy products.
2. **Buyer Experience**
   - Help customers understand products and make better buying decisions.
3. **Merchant Tooling**
   - Build tools that help merchants manage, optimise, and grow their stores.
4. **New Ways to Buy**
   - Explore subscriptions, bundles, marketplaces, reselling, and programmatic purchasing.
5. **Agentic Commerce**
   - Build experiences where AI agents can discover, compare, and transact autonomously.

## ⚖️ Judging Criteria & Demo Format

### Demo Format
- Every team submits a live demo before or at **Code Freeze (16:30)**.
- The **Top 5 highest-scoring teams** present their project live (3 mins each).
- Judges select and award **1st, 2nd, and 3rd place**.

### Judging Criteria
- **Problem**: Is the commerce problem clear and worth solving?
- **Experience**: Buyer, seller, or merchant impact.
- **Execution**: Does the prototype work well and reliably?
- **AI / Grok Bot**: Meaningful and innovative use of AI agents.
- **Commerce Depth**: Real understanding and integration of the commerce stack.
- **Originality**: Fresh or distinctive idea.
- **Impact**: Plausible business or customer value.
